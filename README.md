# Whack a Cat

Keep Mr. Biscuits in the air. Don't let him near the plants.

**Play it:** https://darrinstern-KM.github.io/whack-a-cat/

## How to play

- **Tap the cat** to bonk it back into the air. Combos score more.
- **Tap a plant** and you lose 5 points.
- **Miss** and the cat walks over and pees on a plant. Whack it on the way to save the plant (+3).
- One pee kills a plant. Lose all three and it's game over.
- At 100 points a second cat (Pickles) joins, and at 300 a third (Noodle). Each one can miss and pee on its own.
- About once a minute there's a **commercial break**: Zorp the alien dances to a jingle for 5 seconds while the game is frozen. It only starts while every cat is on its way up, and you can skip it after a second.
- Every so often a **broom** falls. Tap it for 5 seconds of superpowers: the cat floats, your swat reaches much further, points are doubled, plant taps are free and the cat can't land to pee.
- Save your score to the shared leaderboard with any name you like.

### Keyboard

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Space` / `↑` / `W` / `Enter` | Swat the lowest cat. It only connects once the cat drops into the low "swat zone" (dashed line). Also grabs a falling broom once it's in the zone. While powered up, the whole screen is the swat zone. |
| `←` `→` / `A` `D` | Hold while swatting to aim the cat left or right |
| `P` / `Esc` | Pause |
| `M` | Sound on/off |
| `Space` / `Enter` on the menu | Start or play again |

Works with mouse, touch or keyboard. It's a single `index.html` with no dependencies, so you can also just download it and open it in a browser.
